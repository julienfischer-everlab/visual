1 Get started

Create your account

Start with the level of care that suits your health goals. Every Everlab plan gives you a connected view of your health, with deeper testing, clinical support and ongoing care available depending on the plan you choose.

Once you’ve selected your plan, you’ll set up your Everlab account and app:

  • Set up in about 10 minutes on your phone
  • Up to 20 years of records consolidated into one view
  • Your health, family and lifestyle history captured once
  • Wearable and app data connected, if you use one

What to have handy

  • Any recent blood tests, imaging or specialist letters, as files or photos
  • Your Medicare card and private health details
  • The name of your regular GP, so results can be shared back
  • Prescriptions and supplements you take regularly

Everything you add stays between you and your care team. You can invite a partner or a family member to their own account later — family history is easier to fill in together.

After you sign up

  • A welcome message from your Care team, usually the same day
  • Your questionnaire saved as you go, so it can be finished in stages
  • A checklist of anything still missing from your history
  • The app on iOS and Android, with the same view on both

Your first consultation slot opens as soon as there is enough history for a doctor to review — for most people that is a day or two after signing up.

Onboarding consultation

2 Consult

Meet your doctor

Choose or get matched with a doctor trained in preventative health, who has reviewed your history before you join the call. This isn’t a fifteen-minute appointment where you need to explain your background from scratch. It’s:

  • 30 unhurried minutes by video, from anywhere in Australia
  • Your history, family risk and health goals discussed in detail
  • A testing plan tailored to you, with additional tests recommended where your doctor sees a need
  • A recommendation on the membership plan your doctor believes best suits you, and why

Before the call

  • A short questionnaire on symptoms, medications and supplements
  • Your doctor’s profile, so you know who you’re meeting
  • A reminder the day before, with a link that works on a phone or a laptop
  • Somewhere quiet for half an hour — nothing else to prepare

If the fit isn’t right, you can change doctors at any point without repeating yourself. Your history belongs to your account, not to one clinician.

During the consultation

  • A walk through the history you’ve already given, not a repeat of it
  • Time set aside for the questions you came with
  • Your testing plan explained test by test, including why each one is on it
  • Notes written up and added to your record afterwards

Consultations run to time because the reading is done beforehand. If something needs longer, your doctor books the extra time rather than rushing the end of the call.

3 Test

Complete your testing

You book each test in the app and your Care team handles referrals, coordination and anything that needs chasing.

  • Access 1,850+ vetted facilities Australia-wide
  • Testing is selected for you based on your history, risks and doctor consultation
  • Diagnostic and Protocol plans can include advanced bloods, imaging and deeper diagnostics where appropriate
  • Protocol members can access at-home blood collection
  • Results are added to your app as they arrive, with further testing recommended if needed

How booking works

  • Pick a collection centre by suburb and opening hours
  • Fasting and preparation instructions the day before
  • Reschedule in the app if something comes up
  • Your Care team follows up on anything that hasn’t arrived on time

Most blood panels come back within a week. Imaging and the more specialised assays take longer, and the app shows you where each one is up to rather than leaving you to ring around.

When results arrive

  • Each result added to your record as the lab releases it
  • Anything urgent flagged to your doctor the same day
  • Plain-language notes next to the numbers
  • Earlier results shown alongside, so you can see the direction of travel

Nothing is held back until your next consultation. If a result needs acting on before then, your doctor contacts you.

4 Act

Act, with follow-ups included

Your doctor takes you through your results marker by marker. Where a result deserves a closer look, our specialist clinicians review it and your doctor arranges what comes next.

  • A 45-minute results consultation, and a written plan to follow
  • Follow-up consultations included, at no extra cost
  • Referral letters and specialist introductions, if your doctor advises it
  • Your results ready to share with your GP from the app

Between consultations

  • Message your Care team in the app
  • A re-test schedule for the markers worth watching
  • Plan updates as new results arrive
  • An annual review to reset your goals for the year ahead

Nothing sits in a portal waiting to be found. When a result changes something, your doctor tells you what it means and what to do about it.

If something needs attention

  • A review by one of our specialist clinicians before your consultation
  • A referral written for you, with a specialist suggested where we can
  • Your records packaged for whoever you are referred to
  • A follow-up booked to close the loop, rather than left to you to chase

Most findings are managed inside your plan. Where they aren’t, you leave with a referral, the reasoning behind it, and a date to come back.
