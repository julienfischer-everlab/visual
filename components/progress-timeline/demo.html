How it decides

Sizing

Width is measured from the parent with a ResizeObserver. 128px stays reserved at each edge; the space between is where dots rest.

All steps fit at the minimum segment? They spread evenly, first dot on the left safe line, last on the right. Otherwise the segment is exactly the minimum and the track scrolls.

Positioning

First step or not started: first dot on the left safe line. Last step or complete: last dot on the right safe line, its label free to use the safe area.

In between: current and next are kept clear of any fade; the previous step stays clear when there's room for three, and all spare room goes to what's coming.

Overflow

A 128px fade appears only on a side the track runs past. A 28px button sits in the middle of that edge area, shown only when a connector (not a dot or empty track) is beneath its centre; it asks the host to open the full timeline.

Compact

Below 2 × 128 + 2 × min segment (512px by default) the edge areas and two readable steps can't both fit, so it switches to two columns, current and next, with 44px edges.
