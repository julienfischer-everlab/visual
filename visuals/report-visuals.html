Report visuals

V2: each report as a card on its own picture: the title at the left, the report's visual flat in a thin outlined frame, then its button(s). Under each card, three backgrounds: V1, a picture, with the frame showing it dithered; V2, a flat colour, also behind the frame's own picture, dithered in black or white squares on it; V3, a flat colour with only the report's illustration in the frame, in black or white (the illustration shows on V3 only). The frame's outline can be on or off. Save a card's settings as a preset (the first control) to come back to them; your choices and presets stay in this browser.

V1: each report's reveal, as the pop-up it opens in: the title, the report's card, the doctor's words, then the button or buttons. The card is the same for every report, an isometric report with one sheet under it; only its panel changes, a grid of square particles.
