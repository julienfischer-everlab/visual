Report visuals

The card each report reveals, one per report: the same isometric report card with one sheet offset under it, a visual panel and three short lines of report text. Only the panel changes, its picture a grid of square particles, each in a deliberate place. Shown assembled and still; the motion follows once the shapes are agreed.
