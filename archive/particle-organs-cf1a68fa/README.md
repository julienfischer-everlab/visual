## Controls

A top bar exposes **Version**, **Density**, **Particles size**, a
**Flow / Voxels** toggle, and a light/dark switch — the tuning knobs that the
later iteration drops in favour of age, mode, and colour-mode controls.

Its **Version** numbering also differs from the later snapshot: here `4` is the
spatial view, `5` the grid, and `6` the bento, where the later file folds these
into a different order.
