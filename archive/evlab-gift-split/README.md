# Unwrap your Everlab gift · Split

The third version of the gift page. Fold 1 and the transition are identical
to `evlab-gift-unwrap`; fold 2 splits the composition. The glass card covers
only the left half and holds the text: the "Gift received from Matt" line, the
product name, the description, and the "What's included" list. On the right,
outside the card and directly over the photograph, sit the membership
passport card, the "What's next" timeline and the **Claim my gift** button.
The right side rises a beat after the card.

Since this version `index.html` is the same combined page as
`../evlab-gift-unwrap/index.html`, differing only in `DEFAULT_VARIANT`
(`'split'`) and the `<title>`, so this link opens on the split layout. The
**Version** select in the top bar switches to the other two in place. In the
split layout the card is a plain glass panel and the right side sits over the
photograph; in the 01 layout the same right side takes on the glass and joins
the card as its right column.

Everything else (the letter-by-letter message, the circle, the dark theme,
the iPhone frame mode, the query-string parameters, the photograph) is as
documented in `../evlab-gift-unwrap/README.md`.

On phones the card and the right side stack, and the floating **Claim my
gift** button takes over from the one on the right.

## Viewing

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. No server or build step.
